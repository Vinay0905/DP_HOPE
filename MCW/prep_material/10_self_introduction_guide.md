# 🗣️ Self-Introduction Guide

Your self-introduction sets the tone for the entire interview. Since MulticoreWare has two main pathways (**Software Engineer** and **Research Engineer**), here are tailored templates. 

---

## 1. Core Structure of a Great Introduction
A successful self-introduction follows the **SEMR** framework:
1.  **S - Start (Who you are):** Name, education, ECE background.
2.  **E - Experience (Internships):** Highlight Spheruler Solutions (CV/Math) and Zeka Pvt Solutions (AI/APIs).
3.  **M - Major Projects / Achievements:** CodePro (DSA/Compilers) or YOLOv8 (Computer Vision).
4.  **R - Reason (Why MulticoreWare):** Connect your low-level ECE mindset and AI experience to their work on high-performance computing, video compression, and edge AI.

---

> 
> My academic background in ECE gave me a strong appreciation for low-level systems, which led me to focus deeply on software optimization, data structures, and AI deployment.

I believe my combination of low-level ECE understanding, strong DSA fundamentals, and microservices experience makes me a great fit to write high-performance code here. Thank you."

## 4. Key Tips for Delivery
*   **The ECE Hook:** Interviewers will ask, *"Why software/AI coming from ECE?"* 
    *   *Your answer*: ECE teaches you signal processing, math, and how hardware works under the hood (memory registers, caches, processors). High-performance software engineering (what MulticoreWare does) requires understanding how your code interacts with this hardware. You aren't just writing code; you are writing *hardware-aware* code.
*   **Keep it under 2 minutes:** Speak at a steady pace. Do not rush through the numbers (the 3x improvement, 47% security improvement, 8.67 CGPA).
*   **Pause for questions:** At the end, invite them to ask about Spheruler (camera calibration) or CodePro (Rabin-Karp), which directs the conversation to your strongest subjects.
